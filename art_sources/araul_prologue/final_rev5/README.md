# Araoul prologue rev5 archive

This directory preserves the accepted 1672x941 rev5 source plates and the exact
historical production manifest after the V3 art rebuild replaced rev5 at
runtime.

- `araul_prologue_v2_rev5_manifest.json` SHA-256:
  `c4bd038f8318ec28b29940f004433dc86ae7a224d5f8cd28167f0c1ccaac505a`
- The manifest's `runtime_authority: true` field records its status when rev5
  was captured. It is archival provenance and does not supersede the live V3
  manifest.
- The eight 3344x1882 runtime copies and their Godot import sidecars were
  removed after V3 and rev6 acceptance. They remain reproducible from these
  source plates with the upscale/import settings recorded in the manifest and
  recoverable from Git/LFS history where applicable.

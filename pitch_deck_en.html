PingFighter reinvents the classic Pong formula by adding boss AI combat, perk builds, item collection, an explorable hub world, and online PvP — creating an entirely new sub-genre of arcade action.

Players control a paddle to rally a ball against boss paddles, each with unique AI patterns and special attacks. Strategic depth comes from dashes, active items, and perk builds that make every run feel different.

"Pong + Boss Battles + RPG Progression — easy to pick up, impossible to master."

3. Core Gameplay

3.1. The Basics

Control a bottom paddle to rally a ball against a boss paddle at the top. There are no HP bars — matches are pure point-based (first to 5 points wins).

PingFighter has no health system. Victory is determined by a pure point-based rally, just like real table tennis.
RuleDetails
1 PointBall passes the opponent's goal line
VictoryFirst to 5 points (win_goal = 5)
Deuce (1st)4:4 tie → target extends to 6 points
Deuce (2nd)5:5 tie → target extends to 7 points (max)
Match PointSpecial cinematic triggers when one side reaches 4 points

3.2. Signature Mechanic: “Half-Dash” System

The dash is PingFighter's core mechanic. It teleports your paddle to evade or reposition aggressively.

TypeDistanceCostCooldownDetails
Full Dash150px1 Token60 frames (1s)Standard dash. Direction key + Space
Half Dash75px0 TokensSharedAuto-activates when tokens are depleted. Emergency evasion
Chain Dash150px1 Token200ms delayRapid consecutive dashes when multiple tokens are available

Even when dash tokens are depleted, a 50% distance emergency dash is always available. This eliminates complete helplessness — skilled play is always rewarded, and it creates clutch reversal moments that define high-level play.

Behind the Story: The Horned Strawberry

While eating strawberries, the developer discovered one with two tiny horn-like protrusions on top — a bizarre, almost demonic shape. Thinking "this should be PingFighter's mascot," the strawberry was freeze-dried and preserved in resin as a 1/1 edition art piece. This real-life horned strawberry directly inspired the in-game mythic item "Horned Strawberry Mask" — a transformation item with 3 unique skills.

5.C. Gacha System

3-tier distribution: Mythic (5%–30%) / Active (50%) / Passive (45%). Full capsule machine animation (coin insert → spin → capsule drop → reveal). 500 Gold per pull, 40 capsules per session.

Gacha System: Capsule machine with tier-based item reveals

5.D. Downtown (Hub World)

13 building types (7 active) — an explorable pixel-art town with gacha shop, item store, bank, colosseum, minigames, forge, and tavern. NPC dialogue, merchants, and tile-based free movement. The Bodyguard system lets captured arena heroes follow you as companions.

5.E. Online PvP

P2P socket-based multiplayer with host/guest matchmaking, character & stage selection lobby, and recent IP auto-save for quick reconnection.

5.F. Replay System

60fps full-screen capture with async compression (zero frame drops). Sound event auto-recording & playback. Up to 10-minute recordings.

Replay Viewer: Watch and review past matches with full audio
Stage 2: Gator General — Jungle / Swamp arena

5.G. Quest System — 8 Quests

Accept quests at the Downtown Tavern. Combat missions include: Perfect Victory, Speed Run (3 min), No Dash, 15-Rally Streak, and more. Rewards range from 800–2,500 Gold.

5.H. Dynamic Weather — 7 Types

10% chance per round: Breeze, Gale, Fire, Ice, Rain, Hail, Sandstorm. Each weather type changes physics (slippery ice, fire acceleration, rain speed debuff) with full particle systems.

5.I. Colosseum Arena Meta-Loop

Capture → Servant → Bodyguard promotion loop with 15 arena heroes. 3 tiers of seals: Beginner (Quarterfinals), Intermediate (Semifinals), Bodyguard (Finals, permanent). Up to 2 bodyguards equipped, visible as Downtown followers.

5.J. Tutorial

6-chapter progressive tutorial (sub, dash, drive, power smash) + 5 arena-specific tutorials.

5.K. Opening & Stage Intros

Story cutscene with protagonist “Yuian”. 8 unique stage intro videos (OpenCV playback + boss text overlay). 5-scene cinematic: paddle-ball meeting → boss reveal → battle → power-up → final showdown.

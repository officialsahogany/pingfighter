Generate an 8-frame 4x2 pixel-art dash sprite sheet for the front-facing

- muzzle centered

- torso yaw = 0
- shoulders remain front-facing
- no side-facing drift

Dash sequence target:
- frame 1 ready stance

- fast intimidating relocation

- body scale must stay consistent with walk

- do not hide the face
- do not lose the dangling eye thread

- 16-bit retro pixel art
- thick black pixel outlines

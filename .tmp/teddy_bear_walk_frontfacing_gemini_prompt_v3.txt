Create one 8-frame 4x2 sprite sheet of the SAME teddy bear doing a PURE front-facing lateral walk for a retro pixel-art boss game.

The torso stays front-facing, but the LEGS, ARMS, and BODY HEIGHT must visibly animate across the 8 frames. This is NOT an idle gallery.

SQUARED-TORSO RULE (keep):
- Shoulders stay level and horizontal.
- Chest / belly / heart patch face the viewer dead-on.
- Face stays centered, both ears visible and symmetric.
- Head does NOT tilt sideways, does NOT yaw left or right.
- Torso does NOT rotate toward either side.
- NO 3/4 angle. NO profile angle. NO side-view.

MOTION RULES (must be visible between frames):
- Legs visibly ALTERNATE: on contact frames one leg is clearly forward / planted while the other is back / lifted. Swap sides across the cycle.
- Body height visibly BOBS up and down across the 8 frames. Between the lowest-bob frame and the highest-bob frame the entire bear should move roughly 6-10 pixels vertically at source-size.
- Arms swing IN FRONT of the body, alternating: when the left leg is forward, the right paw swings slightly forward; when the right leg is forward, the left paw swings slightly forward. Swing is small and plush, but clearly different between frames.
- Ears bounce vertically with the body bob.
- Pink bow bounces vertically with the body bob.
- Dangling right eye thread swings slightly.
- Plush belly wobbles softly.
- Pelvis may slide horizontally left/right slightly (translation, NOT rotation) to show weight transfer.

This is PURE front-facing plush waddling walk:
- Front-view paper-doll teddy, actively waddling in place.
- Lateral travel across the screen comes from leg cycling and weight shift, not from torso rotation.

Per-frame pose plan (frames MUST differ visibly):
- f1 LEFT-contact: left foot forward and planted, right foot back, body at mid height, right paw swings slightly forward in front of belly, left paw slightly back.
- f2 LEFT-pass / bob-up: left leg pushing off, right leg lifting, body at HIGH bob (visibly higher than f1), arms passing near neutral.
- f3 PASSING: both legs near center under the bear, body at LOW bob (visibly lower than f1), arms passing neutral.
- f4 RIGHT-contact: right foot forward and planted, left foot back, body at mid height, left paw swings slightly forward in front of belly, right paw slightly back (mirror of f1).
- f5 RIGHT-pass / bob-up: right leg pushing off, left leg lifting, body at HIGH bob (visibly higher than f4), arms passing near neutral.
- f6 PASSING: both legs near center under the bear, body at LOW bob, arms passing neutral (mirror of f3).
- f7 returning LEFT-contact with slight variation (small arm / ear offset vs f1).
- f8 loop-back close to f1 but with the dangling eye thread and bow in a slightly different swing phase.

Character design to preserve in every frame:
- plush cocoa-brown teddy bear, chibi stuffed-toy proportions
- left button eye with red X stitch
- right damaged dangling eye on a thread
- large pink gingham bow on the head
- cream neck ribbon
- stitched heart belly patch
- safety pin charm on chest
- small black bow accent on one paw

- cute but slightly uncanny menhera-plush mood

- no visible vertical bob across the cycle
- legs not alternating

- torso tilt, torso lean, shoulder tilt, or pelvis rotation
- side-facing walk, 3/4 walk, angled walk, rotation chart
- humanized runway gait
- dance loop

- chibi proportions
- thick black pixel outlines
- flat limited-saturation palette
- clean hard-edged pixels
- NO painterly rendering
- NO soft anime illustration
- NO photorealism
- pure flat white background (#FFFFFF)
- no grid lines, no borders, no dividers, no labels between cells
- each cell exactly equal size, character centered

Gameplay-scale readability is mandatory from the first generation.
Face, button eye, dangling eye thread, bow, ribbon, heart patch, and paws must all stay readable at small in-game size.

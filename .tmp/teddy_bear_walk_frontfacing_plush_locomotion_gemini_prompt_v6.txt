Create one 8-frame 4x2 sprite sheet of the SAME teddy bear doing a FRONT-FACING plush locomotion cycle for a retro pixel-art boss game.

This is a design pivot away from a literal side-step walk.
The teddy still moves laterally in gameplay, but the animation language is:
- front-facing plush locomotion
- hop-step / bounce-step / waddling bob
- toy-like sideways travel while the bear keeps facing the viewer

This is NOT:
- a side-profile walk
- a 3/4 walk
- a rotation chart
- a human gait
- a backward-walking read

Core body rule:
- face looks straight at the viewer in all 8 frames
- eyes centered forward
- muzzle centered forward
- head yaw = 0
- torso yaw = 0
- shoulders horizontal
- chest and heart patch face the viewer dead-on

Character identity to preserve:
- plush cocoa-brown teddy bear, chibi stuffed-toy proportions
- LEFT eye = attached black button with red X stitch
- RIGHT eye = empty socket with a small detached black button hanging below it on a short white thread
- large pink gingham bow on the head
- cream neck ribbon under the chin
- stitched heart belly patch
- safety pin on upper chest above the heart
- small black bow accent on one paw
- bandage patches and plush stitching
- cute but slightly uncanny menhera-plush mood

Locomotion brief:
- think plush hop-step, bounce-step, waddling bob
- clear up/down body bob
- side-to-side plush weight shift
- feet / legs change position clearly between frames, but not like a realistic human march
- some frames can have both feet closer together during the bounce
- paws can lift slightly in alternating rhythm
- ears and head bow bounce with the body
- dangling eye thread swings subtly
- belly plush mass wobbles softly

Sequence idea:
- compress
- little rise
- soft landing on one side
- rebound
- opposite-side repeat
- loop cleanly

Hard reject:
- any side-facing or 3/4 read
- any stable rightward or leftward gaze bias
- a literal backward-walking impression during left travel
- same pose repeated 8 times
- static idle gallery
- human runway gait
- dance loop

Style:
- 16-bit retro pixel art
- thick black pixel outlines
- flat limited-saturation palette
- clean hard-edged pixels
- no painterly rendering
- no soft anime illustration
- pure white background
- no cell borders or dividers
- full body visible in every frame

Deliver one strongest candidate first.

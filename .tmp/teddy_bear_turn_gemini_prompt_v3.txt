Generate an 8-frame 4x2 pixel-art turn / facing-transition sprite sheet for
the Stage 3 menhera-themed teddy bear boss.

This is a remake after the previous visible turn felt unnatural in runtime.

Goal:
- more natural
- less exaggerated
- still clearly readable as a short direction-change accent

This is NOT a side-profile turn chart.
This is NOT a multi-angle rotation sheet.
The teddy must remain front-facing in every frame.

Core turn concept:
- front-facing plush hesitation / pivot accent
- small natural head tilt and return
- ears lag and flap softly with the head motion
- tiny plush body rebound
- optional small paw adjustment
- not a big cartoon "no-no" wag
- not a spin
- not a walk loop

Most important rule:
- the face and accessories must NEVER mirror or swap sides between frames
- LEFT eye stays the red X button in every frame
- RIGHT eye stays the empty dark socket with the detached hanging button
  on a thin white thread in every frame
- do not flip the face, do not expose "the other side" of the face,
  do not reinterpret the character as mirrored on the bottom row

Identity:
- plush cocoa-brown chibi teddy bear
- LEFT eye is a dark button with a red X stitch
- RIGHT eye is an empty dark socket with a small detached black button
  hanging below on a thin white thread
- large pink gingham bow stays on the head
- cream neck ribbon stays clearly visible
- heart belly patch stays fully visible and centered
- small silver safety pin stays on the upper chest
- small black bow stays on the left paw
- same teddy in every frame, same accessory positions, same body scale

Natural turn sequence target:
- frame 1 neutral walk-adjacent entry
- frame 2 slight head tilt begins
- frame 3 gentle one-side peak tilt, ear on that side lowers slightly,
  opposite ear lags upward
- frame 4 rebound back through center
- frame 5 slight opposite tilt begins
- frame 6 gentle opposite-side peak tilt with soft ear lag
- frame 7 settle back through center
- frame 8 neutral recovery ready to return to walk

Important:
- motion should feel organic and plush, not twitchy or overly theatrical
- frames 2, 4, 5, 7, and 8 must still be clearly distinct
- face acting may change slightly: blink, tiny mouth change, soft sleepy look,
  or a tiny paw-near-face adjustment in one brief frame only
- any paw-near-face cue must stay subtle and must NOT hide the dangling-eye read
- ears themselves must visibly shift shape / height, not only use motion lines
- head tilt must be readable but modest
- do NOT over-yaw the head enough to reveal the opposite face side

Composition:
- pure white background
- no grid lines
- no labels
- no borders
- no cell dividers
- no printed rectangles around frames
- one unbroken white background between and inside cells

Style:
- 16-bit retro pixel art
- thick black outlines
- flat limited palette
- hard-edged pixels

Hard reject:
- side profile or 3/4 turn chart
- same pose repeated 8 times
- turn reads like walk loop or idle gallery
- head motion missing
- ear motion missing
- face/accessories mirror or swap sides between frames
- dangling eye becomes a flat face button
- bow moves off the head
- cream ribbon shrinks to near-invisible
- safety pin leaves upper chest
- black paw bow disappears
- heart belly patch gets clipped or covered
- any printed border, panel line, frame outline, or divider appears

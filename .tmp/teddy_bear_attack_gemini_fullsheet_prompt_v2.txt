Generate an 8-frame 4x2 pixel-art attack sprite sheet for a front-facing
Stage 3 menhera-themed teddy bear boss.

This is a targeted retake of Gemini attack v1.
The v1 sequence succeeded on identity and anticipatory arc, but the strike
read as a slight rightward jab / side hit.

This retake must fix that.

Core correction:
- the teddy is NOT punching to the right
- the teddy is NOT looking to the right while striking
- the attack must read as a centered, downward / downward-forward plush smack
  toward the ball in front of the teddy
- the hit direction should feel like "bringing the paws down onto the ball"
  rather than "jabbing sideways"

Required attack arc:
- frame 1 ready
- frame 2 paws gather inward
- frame 3 compact coil
- frame 4 max charge
- frame 5 launch downward-forward from the centered frontal pose
- frame 6 strongest impact frame, centered and downward
- frame 7 follow-through downward
- frame 8 recovery

Identity:

- LEFT eye is a dark button with a red X stitch

  hanging below on a thin white thread
- large pink gingham bow on the head
- cream neck ribbon under the head
- heart belly patch
- small silver safety pin on upper chest
- small black bow on the left paw
- same accessory positions, same body scale, same teddy in every frame

Front-facing lock:
- face remains centered
- muzzle remains centered
- head yaw = 0
- torso yaw = 0
- zero side-profile
- zero visible rightward jab pose

Motion:
- double-paw plush smack / clap strike
- compact stuffed-toy spring
- short anticipatory prep
- clear centered impact
- visible prep, visible release, visible recovery

Style:
- 16-bit retro pixel art
- thick black outlines
- flat limited palette
- hard-edged pixels
- pure white background
- no grid lines, no labels, no borders, no cell dividers

- same pose repeated 8 times
- frame 6 not clearly strongest impact

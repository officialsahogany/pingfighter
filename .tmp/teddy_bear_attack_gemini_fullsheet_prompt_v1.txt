Generate an 8-frame 4x2 pixel-art attack sprite sheet for a front-facing
Stage 3 menhera-themed teddy bear boss.

This is a contact-based ball-hit attack for gameplay that will likely use
SHORT anticipatory runtime triggering.
So the sequence must read:
- frame 1 ready
- frame 2 paws gather inward
- frame 3 compact coil
- frame 4 max charge
- frame 5 launch
- frame 6 strongest impact frame
- frame 7 short follow-through
- frame 8 recovery

The prep must be short and meaningful, not dead idle posing.
The impact must be clearly stronger than the other frames.

Character identity:
- plush cocoa-brown chibi teddy bear, unmistakably teddy species
- LEFT eye is a dark button with a red X stitch
- RIGHT eye is an empty dark socket with a small detached black button
  hanging below on a thin white thread
- large pink gingham bow on the head
- cream neck ribbon under the head
- heart belly patch
- small silver safety pin on upper chest
- small black bow on the left paw
- same teddy in every frame, same accessory positions, same body scale

Motion concept:
- double-paw plush smack / clap strike toward the camera
- compact stuffed-toy spring, not human martial arts
- clear prep -> strike -> recovery
- front-facing in every frame
- zero side profile
- zero 3/4 turn

Style:
- 16-bit retro pixel art
- thick black outlines
- flat limited palette
- hard-edged pixels
- pure white background
- no grid lines, no labels, no borders

Hard reject:
- same pose repeated 8 times
- frame 6 not clearly strongest impact
- painterly or soft illustrated style
- dangling eye becomes a flat face button
- head bow moves off the head
- safety pin moves off the upper chest
- any frame becomes side-facing or 3/4
